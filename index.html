How It Works

A simple, 3-step process to make a massive impact.

1. Post Surplus Food

Donors list available surplus quickly, adding details like servings and expiration time.

2. NGOs Claim & Collect

NGOs track nearby listings, claim the portions they need, and arrange quick pickups.

3. Nourish Communities

Perfectly good food reaches those who need it most, spreading joy and community.
